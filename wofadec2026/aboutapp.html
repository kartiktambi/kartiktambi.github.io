World Forum of Accountants 2026

The World Forum of Accountants (WOFA), 2026, scheduled to be held in Visakhapatnam, India from 16th to 18th December 2026, is envisioned as a global confluence of financial leaders, policymakers, and innovators united by a common mission — to shape a future where progress is purposeful, people-centric, and sustainable.

Building on the strong legacy of its earlier editions, WOFA 2026 comes at a defining moment for India as the nation advances toward its Viksit Bharat 2047 vision. The Forum will highlight the transformative role of the accounting and finance profession in enabling trust-based governance, fostering digital inclusion, promoting ethical AI adoption, and driving climate-conscious growth.

By encouraging collaboration, facilitating knowledge exchange, and upholding the highest standards of professional excellence, WOFA, 2026 inspires accountants to act as vital connectors between governments, businesses, and society. It celebrates the profession’s unique ability to translate expertise into impactful global solutions, contributing to a future defined by integrity, sustainability, and shared prosperity.

About This Year WOFA’s Theme

The global financial landscape is being reshaped by rapid technological advancements, evolving regulations, and growing expectations of transparency and sustainability. In this dynamic environment, the accounting profession is moving beyond its traditional role to become a key driver of trust, innovation, and transformation.

The theme, “Redefining Excellence: Technology | Trust | Transformation”, captures this evolution and the changing expectations from the profession. It highlights the transformative role of technology in enhancing efficiency, insights, and decision-making; the enduring importance of trust as the cornerstone of financial systems and public confidence; and the profession’s expanding responsibility in driving resilience, sustainability, and inclusive growth.

The theme also reflects the Institute’s commitment to embracing innovation, strengthening professional credibility, and fostering meaningful global collaboration. It highlights our vision of building a future-ready profession that is agile, ethical, and equipped to respond effectively to emerging opportunities and challenges in an increasingly interconnected world.
