Explore Visakhapatnam

The City of Destiny

A coastal city with a world of character

Fondly known as Vizag, Visakhapatnam blends natural beauty and cultural heritage with growing technological and industrial ambition. A major natural harbour on India’s eastern coast, the city offers an inspiring setting for WOFA 2026 and its spirit of innovation, sustainability and global collaboration.

Places to Explore

From serene coastlines to hilltop views and cultural landmarks.

9 destinations
Beach

Rushikonda Beach

Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

Nestled beneath scenic Rushikonda Hill, this beautiful stretch of coast pairs lush green slopes with a tranquil shoreline. Relax by the waves or explore supervised water-sport activities.

Approx. 1 km from venue
Spiritual

Sri Venkateswara Swamy Konda Temple

Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

A well-known spiritual landmark dedicated to Lord Venkateswara. Its distinctive, steep pyramid-like entrance and peaceful surroundings make it a memorable place to pause and reflect.

Approx. 1–2 km from venue
Spiritual

ISKCON Temple Visakhapatnam

Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

A devotional setting known for its calm atmosphere, offering visitors a peaceful place for meditation, spiritual reflection and a quiet break from the city.

Approx. 4–5 km from venue
Spiritual

Kali Temple

RK Beach, Visakhapatnam

Dedicated to Goddess Kali, this much-visited temple is a notable spiritual and cultural landmark. Its Vijaydashami celebrations draw devotees and visitors throughout the year.

Approx. 5–6 km from venue
Wildlife

Indira Gandhi Zoological Park

Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

Spread across 625 acres, this large zoological park is home to a wide variety of species. Butterfly and moth sections and a mini-train ride add to the family-friendly experience.

Approx. 6–7 km from venue
Hilltop Park

Kailasagiri

Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

A scenic hilltop park with sweeping views of the city, coastline and surrounding greenery. It is a popular spot to unwind, enjoy the panorama and take in the landscape.

Approx. 9–10 km from venue
Beach

Ramakrishna Beach

Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

Popularly known as RK Beach, this lively coastline is loved for its relaxed seaside atmosphere, recreation and sunset views—a great place to slow down after a busy day.

Approx. 10–11 km from venue
Maritime Heritage

INS Kurusura Submarine Museum

Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

Explore a decommissioned Indian Navy submarine and discover stories of naval history, submarine technology and life beneath the sea at this distinctive maritime attraction.

Approx. 11–12 km from venue
Spiritual & Heritage

Simhachalam Temple

Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

Also known as Sri Varaha Lakshmi Narasimha Temple, this revered shrine features grand gateways and courtyards, with architectural influences from Kalinga, Chalukya and Chola traditions.

Approx. 35–40 km from venue
Hill Station

Araku Valley

Near Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

A picturesque hill retreat known for green landscapes, coffee plantations and vibrant tribal culture. Local heritage and the scenic train journey add to the appeal of a longer day trip.

Approx. 130–135 km from venue
